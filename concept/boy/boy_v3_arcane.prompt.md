## ⚠️ This departs from the locked style

`docs/treatment/style.md` is LOCKED as Mid-Century Print / tilt-dab. This prompt
crosses three of its rules on purpose, at Ian's direction (v2 read too muted and
too flat):

1. **Modelled form.** The doc says shapes are flat and unmodelled, and shading
   comes only from light plus tilt dabs. This asks for volume.
2. **Saturation discipline.** The doc's field is chalked back with one hot accent;
   this spends colour everywhere and drives shadows into saturated teal/indigo.
3. **Rim light.** Not in the doc at all — the spec is one hard sun, no fill, no
   edge light.

None of that matters for a concept frame. It matters if this becomes the target,
because `MCM_Toon` as built cannot produce deep coloured shadows or rim light
without changes. Worth an A/B against v2 on the sq020-sh020 look-dev frame before
either becomes canon.
