CHUNKY DIRECTIONAL GOUACHE STROKES INSIDE FLAT SILHOUETTES.

Every form is a flat, unmodelled graphic shape — no volumetric rendering, no soft

But every one of those shapes is filled with a loaded brush, and you can see it.
Fat, deliberate, visible strokes. Each stroke is one flat facet of pigment sitting
at a slightly different tilt from its neighbour, so the light breaks across the
surface in stroke-shaped patches instead of blending. Adjacent strokes disagree
slightly in hue and value — a leg is not one denim color, it is a dozen strokes of
