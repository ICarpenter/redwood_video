# guns — video script (skeleton)

Working document for the ideation/writing phases. Each section block gets a
visual concept, then breaks down into shots that land in `../shotlist.csv`.
Frames come from `../sections.csv` — cut points should snap to beats via
`../beatmap.csv`.

## Themes on the table (from the lyrics — candidates, not decisions)

- Heartland decay: rural America, struggle, quiet rebellion talked but not acted.
- Commerce of violence: guns sold like consumer goods; "peace of mind" as a
  product; the chorus is a sales pitch.
- "In the dead hand they all trust" — dollar-bill parody; dead hand = the
  market, inheritance, or the NRA's cold dead hands. Rich double meaning.
- The lawman verse: fear on both sides of a door.
- Verse 3 turn: Austin, a rifle passed on, stocks up while kids are "in a
  slump" — the ledger where profit and loss are counted in different units.
- Tone question to settle: satire, elegy, or deadpan both?
